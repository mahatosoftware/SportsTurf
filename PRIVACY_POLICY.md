# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 10, 2026

**SportsTurf** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by SportsTurf.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**SportsTurf** is designed as a local-only application. 

- **Personal Data:** We do not knowingly collect, transmit, or store any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) on external servers.
- **Match Data:** Any match data, scores, player names, or statistics you enter into the application are stored locally on your device using a secure local database (SQLite). This data remains on your device and is not shared with us or any third parties.

## 2. Permissions

The application may request certain permissions to function correctly:

- **Storage:** To save and retrieve your match history database on your local device.

## 3. Third-Party Services

SportsTurf does not currently integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud storage services that collect user data.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored locally on your device. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device secure to protect your local data.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page or within the application.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
